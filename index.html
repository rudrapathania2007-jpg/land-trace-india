URBAN EXPANSION

Growth does not stop at the city boundary.

As towns and cities expand, development often moves into their surrounding rural fringe. Agricultural parcels can be converted into housing colonies, roads, industrial areas and other built-up land.

The challenge is not simply knowing that change exists. We need to know where it happened, how quickly it happened, what land was affected, and what the pattern looks like over time.

A NATIONAL PERSPECTIVE

Urban expansion is a spatial story.

India's metropolitan regions and fast-growing cities create expanding peri-urban zones where rural and urban land uses overlap.

Digital mapping provides a repeatable way to study these transition zones rather than relying only on ground observations.
